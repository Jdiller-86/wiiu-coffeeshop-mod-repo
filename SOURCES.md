# Mod sources and archive checks

This ledger covers all installable entries in `games/*/game.json`. The ZIPs are hosted by their creators or existing community hosts; this repository contains metadata only. Every ZIP has been downloaded and checked for a root `content/` and/or `aoc/` directory, byte size, SHA-256, and unsafe paths. Original entries were audited 2026-10-03; new entries and the Snowy SMBU fix were audited 2026-10-04. An archive check does not prove successful play on physical hardware or every region.

The older [Funtum CoffeeShop repository](https://github.com/ItsFuntum/funtum-coffeeshop-repo) supplies some community-hosted archives and links to the original authors. We do not mirror or repackage creator files. If an author wants an entry corrected or removed, open a repository issue.
